\chapter*{Overview}

This blueprint documents a machine-checked Lean~4 model of the Wikifunctions / WikiLambda object
system, built \emph{ground-up from the spec}. Its organising principle: \textbf{every value is a
\fnmodel{Z1/ZObjects}{\textsf{Z1}}/ZObject}, and the type layer is \emph{generated} from the authoritative schemata rather
than hand-written. The model lives under \repo{Wikifunctions/Model}{\texttt{Wikifunctions/Model/}}
and is Mathlib-free.

Only two things are hand-written --- the \wf{Z1}/ZObject inductive and the \wf{Z6}/\wf{Z9}
terminals it bottoms out in (Chapter~\ref{ch:zobject}). Every other built-in type is emitted by a
compiler from its definition (Chapter~\ref{ch:types}). Each node links to its exact Lean source
and to the Function-model section or function-schemata file it is derived from; no structural or
behavioural claim is made without that spec citation.

\paragraph{The authorities cited.} The prose
\href{https://www.wikifunctions.org/wiki/Wikifunctions:Function_model}{Function model} (concepts),
the machine-enforced
\href{https://gitlab.wikimedia.org/repos/abstract-wiki/wikifunctions/function-schemata}{function-schemata}
(structural validity), and the live ZObjects (via the \texttt{wikilambda\_fetch} API, the ground
truth for community types). Where the model makes a simplification the spec leaves open, it is
marked \emph{(editorial)}.

\chapter{Z1/ZObjects --- the hand-written substrate}\label{ch:zobject}

The whole model rests on one inductive type and a few accessors, transcribed directly from the
spec's definition of a ZObject and its normal form.

\begin{definition}[ZObject]\label{def:zobject}
  \lean{Wikifunctions.Model.ZObject}\leanok
  Derived verbatim from \fnmodel{Z1/ZObjects}{Function model \S~Z1/ZObjects} (``A ZObject consists
  of a list of Key/value pairs \ldots \wf{Z6}/String and \wf{Z9}/Reference are terminal values;
  they don't expand further'') and \fnmodel{Normal_form}{\S~Normal form} (``a tree where all
  leaves are either \wf{Z6}/String or \wf{Z9}/Reference''): a \texttt{ZObject} is either a terminal
  \texttt{str} leaf or a \texttt{node} --- an ordered list of key/value pairs. Two constructors;
  everything else (\wf{Z4}, \wf{Z8}, \wf{Z7}, \wf{Z881}) is a shape of \texttt{node}.
  Source: \leansrc{Wikifunctions/Model/ZObject.lean}{49}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Accessors and terminals]\label{def:accessors}
  \uses{def:zobject}
  \lean{Wikifunctions.Model.ZObject.type?}\leanok
  Key lookup (\texttt{get?}), the type key (\texttt{type?} \(=\) the \wf{Z1}K1 value; §Z1/ZObjects:
  ``every ZObject must have a key Z1K1/type''), and the two terminals as smart constructors:
  \texttt{string} \(= \{\)\wf{Z1}K1{:}\wf{Z6}, \wf{Z6}K1{:}\(s\}\) and \texttt{reference} \(=
  \{\)\wf{Z1}K1{:}\wf{Z9}, \wf{Z9}K1{:}\(zid\}\), with recognizers.
  Source: \leansrc{Wikifunctions/Model/ZObject.lean}{106}.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Faithful to the spec's normal-form example]\label{lem:nattwo}
  \uses{def:zobject}
  \lean{Wikifunctions.Model.ZObject.natTwo}\leanok
  \texttt{natTwo} reproduces the \fnmodel{Normal_form}{§Normal-form} example \emph{definitionally}
  (\texttt{rfl}): the natural number 2 is \texttt{\{Z1K1:\{Z1K1:Z9,\,Z9K1:Z10\},
  Z10K1:\{Z1K1:Z6,\,Z6K1:"2"\}\}}. This is the load-bearing faithfulness check for the substrate.
  Source: \leansrc{Wikifunctions/Model/ZObject.lean}{149}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{definition}[Well-formedness]\label{def:wf}
  \uses{def:zobject}
  \lean{Wikifunctions.Model.ZObject.WellFormed}\leanok
  The inductive is deliberately permissive --- as the dynamically-typed deployed system is, where
  validators can be skipped --- so validity is a \emph{separate, decidable} predicate \texttt{wf}:
  a \wf{Z1}K1 key, no duplicate keys, and leaves that are \wf{Z6}/\wf{Z9} terminals
  (\fnmodel{Z1/ZObjects}{§Z1/ZObjects}: ``every ZObject must have a key Z1K1/type \ldots every Key
  can only appear once''). \texttt{natTwo} and the terminals check well-formed; a bare leaf, a node
  without \wf{Z1}K1, and a duplicate-key node are rejected.
  Source: \leansrc{Wikifunctions/Model/ZObject.lean}{205}.
\end{definition}

\chapter{The generated type layer}\label{ch:types}

Above the substrate, the built-in types are not written by hand --- they are compiled from their
definitions, so the type layer is faithful to the schemata by construction.

\begin{definition}[The type compiler]\label{def:codegen}
  \uses{def:zobject}
  \repo{codegen/gen\_type.py}{\texttt{codegen/gen\_type.py}} reads each type's authoritative
  definition --- the function-schemata \schema{Z4}-style YAML for built-ins, or the live \wf{Z4}
  (\texttt{wikilambda\_fetch}) for community types, both reduced to \(\{\)type ZID, ordered data
  keys\(\}\) --- and emits, on the \texttt{ZObject} substrate, a smart constructor, per-key
  accessors, an \texttt{isZxx} recognizer, and a \texttt{by decide} self-check that the constructor
  and recognizer agree. The schema \emph{is} the spec, so generation removes per-type
  interpretation entirely.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The built-in inventory]\label{def:types}
  \uses{def:codegen}
  All \textbf{29 built-in types} --- every function-schemata \texttt{CANONICAL} type except the
  hand-written \wf{Z1}/\wf{Z6}/\wf{Z9} --- are generated into
  \repo{Wikifunctions/Model/Types}{\texttt{Wikifunctions/Model/Types/}} (\wf{Z2}, \wf{Z3}, \wf{Z4},
  \wf{Z5}, \wf{Z7}, \wf{Z8}, \wf{Z14}, \wf{Z16}, \wf{Z17}, \wf{Z18}, \wf{Z22}, \wf{Z40}, \ldots).
  All from the schemata YAML except \wf{Z23}/Nothing (live \wf{Z4} fallback); all build green with
  their self-checks. The community numeric type \wf{Z19677}/Rational is generated the same way from
  its live \wf{Z4}. \emph{(editorial)} The current \texttt{isZxx} checks key \emph{presence}; the
  schema's enum/type constraints (e.g.\ \wf{Z40}K1 \(\in \{\)\wf{Z41}, \wf{Z42}\(\}\)) are the next
  increment.
\end{definition}
